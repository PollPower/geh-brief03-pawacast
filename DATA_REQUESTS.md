## 1. AMDA — Africa Minigrid Developers Association

**To:** communications@africamda.org (ask them to forward to the benchmarking/data team)

**Subject:** Request: anonymised site-level consumption data for an open mini-grid demand model

Dear AMDA team,

I'm writing from Poll Power Limited (Nairobi). We're building PawaCast, an open model that gives mini-grid developers a demand range for a site before it is built, for the Global Energy Hackathon (30 Oct – 1 Nov 2026) and beyond.

Your Benchmarking Africa's Minigrids reports are the backbone of our baselines. What we can't find anywhere is site-level data to test against. Would AMDA be willing to share, or to ask members to share, an anonymised extract with, per site:

- country and district (or a coarse grid cell — no exact coordinates needed)
- commissioning month and number of active customers
- monthly kWh sold per customer, ideally split households / businesses

We would agree to a data-sharing agreement of your choosing: no site or developer names published, results reported only in aggregate, and the data used solely for model testing. We'd credit AMDA in everything we publish, and share the resulting model and validation openly with members.

Even a small sample (20–50 sites) would make a real difference. If timing allows a reply before 30 October that would be ideal, but we'd welcome it afterwards too.

Kind regards,
Garrett de Rosset
Founder, PollPower · Poll Power Limited · pollpower.energy

## 2. CrossBoundary Innovation Lab / Energy 4 Impact

**To:** communications@crossboundary.com (ask them to forward to the Mini-Grid Innovation Lab)

**Subject:** Following up your 2019 "Predicting consumption" insight — open demand model

Dear CrossBoundary Innovation Lab team,

Your 2019 Innovation Insight on predicting mini-grid consumption recommended building density, distance to roads and local economic activity as predictors. We've built exactly that kind of model from open data (Overture Maps buildings and places, VIIRS night lights, GridFinder) and are entering it in the Global Energy Hackathon, 30 Oct – 1 Nov 2026.

Our gap is held-out testing: there is no open site-level metered dataset. Would you consider sharing an anonymised extract from the Innovation Lab sites — per site: country, district or coarse location, commissioning month, customer count, and monthly kWh per customer?

We're happy to sign a data-sharing agreement, publish nothing that identifies a site or developer, and share results and code with you first.

Kind regards,
Garrett de Rosset
Founder, PollPower · Poll Power Limited · pollpower.energy

## 3a. Professor Erik Ahlgren (Chalmers), co-author with Dr Yibeltal Wassie

**To:** erik.ahlgren@chalmers.se (corresponding author)
**Subject:** Request for site data behind your Omorate and Tum mini-grid study (Energy Policy, 2024)

Dear Professor Ahlgren,

I read your 2024 Energy Policy paper with Dr Wassie, "Understanding the load profiles and electricity consumption patterns of PV mini-grid customers in rural off-grid East Africa: A data-driven study", with great interest. The Omorate and Tum results are among the very few published measurements of mini-grid demand at named sites with customer numbers and commissioning dates.

We are building PawaCast, an open model that estimates mini-grid demand for a site before it is built, and we are entering it in the Global Energy Hackathon (30 October – 1 November 2026). We are using your two sites as held-out test sites: the model is never tuned on them.

Would you and Dr Wassie be able to share the monthly (or daily) site-level consumption series behind Table 1 and Section 3.1.3, together with the active customer counts over time? Anonymised or aggregated data is entirely fine. It would let us test the growth curve after connection as well as the overall level.

We will cite the paper and any dataset, use the data only for validation, and send you our results. I would be grateful if you could copy Dr Wassie in.

With thanks,
Garrett de Rosset
Founder, PollPower · Poll Power Limited · pollpower.energy
